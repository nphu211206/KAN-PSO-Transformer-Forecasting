# Cover Letter

Dear Editor,

We submit "Training Consistency and Grid-Update Stability in Metaheuristic-Tuned
Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks for Transformer-Temperature Forecasting" for
consideration in the *Journal of Engineering and Applied Science*.

The manuscript addresses a practical engineering question: whether KAN-specific
configuration search is reliable enough for transformer-temperature forecasting.
Using 380 frozen experiment records, independent pilot and canonical protocols,
uniform-checkpoint K09 diagnostics and integrity audits, we show that validation-consistent KAN training can
materially improve a pilot result, while DLinear remains stronger on the
canonical ETTh1/ETTh2 confirmation. We also identify Grid Update instability and
show that resetting Adam state alone is not a sufficient remedy. The paper's
main contribution is a reproducible engineering reliability framework,
including negative findings, event-level intervention evidence, prediction and
residual diagnostics, compute measurements and deployment safeguards. This
positioning is distinct from a generic claim that a new hybrid
model outperforms established baselines.

The work is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and uses public
benchmark data. The author has reviewed and approved this letter and the final
manuscript.

Sincerely,

Nguyen Phu Toan
