## Journal fit and current journal facts

- The scope explicitly includes electrical engineering, artificial and machine
  intelligence, electric power engineering, engineering mathematics,
  computational and stochastic methods, optimization, modelling and
  simulation. The present paper is directly in scope.
- JEAS uses single-blind peer review: reviewers know author names and
  affiliations. Research articles are generally reviewed by two to three
  experts.
- The current journal page reports a median 9 days to first decision. This is a
  descriptive journal metric, not a guarantee.
- The current journal page labels the journal open access and states that it is
  free to publish open access. Confirm the final fee state in the submission
  portal because funding agreements and collection policies can change.
- Because review is single-blind, anonymizing author identity is not the default
  requirement. The live portal must still be checked for any special collection
  instructions.

## Required manuscript content

- Title should include the research design when appropriate.
- Title page must list full author names, institutional addresses and the
  corresponding author.
- LLMs do not qualify as authors. Any LLM use must be documented in Methods or
  another suitable section.
- Abstract should summarize aim and findings, minimize abbreviations and contain
  no citations.
- Three to ten keywords.
- Introduction: background, aims, literature summary and necessity.
- Results: findings and any statistical analysis in text/tables/figures.
- Discussion: implications in context, limitations, and practical/operational
  issues for methodology papers.
- Conclusions: clear conclusions and field relevance.
- Methods/Experimental: aim, design, setting, materials/data, complete processes
  and statistical analysis.
- A list of abbreviations when abbreviations are used.

## Mandatory declarations

Under a `Declarations` heading, include:

- Availability of data and materials
- Competing interests
- Funding
- Authors' contributions
- Acknowledgements
- Authors' information (optional)

Irrelevant mandatory sections must still be present and say `Not applicable`.
Public datasets supporting conclusions should be cited with a persistent
identifier where one exists.

## Figures

- Figure title: maximum 15 words; legend: maximum 300 words.
- Titles/legends belong in the manuscript, not inside the graphic.
- Multi-panel figures must be one composite file.
- Number and cite figures in first-mention order.
- Crop whitespace; incorporate keys into the graphic.
- Maximum 10 MB per figure.
- Accepted: EPS, PDF, Word, PowerPoint, TIFF, JPEG, PNG, BMP and CDX.
- Target 600 px standard or 1200 px high-resolution web width.
- PDF dimensions: 85 mm half-width or 170 mm full-width; maximum height 225 mm.
- Approximately 300 dpi at final size; lines wider than 0.25 pt; embedded fonts.

## Tables

- Number and cite sequentially with Arabic numerals.
- Place normal-sized tables at the relevant manuscript location.
- Title maximum 15 words; legend maximum 300 words.
- Use editable Word table objects, not figures or spreadsheets.
- No color or shading.
- Do not use commas as numerical thousands separators.
- Oversized tabular data may be supplied as XLS or CSV additional files.

## References and files

- References follow Basic Springer style; web resources belong in the numbered
  reference list with access date.
- Footnotes must not be used for citations.
- Editable manuscript files are required after acceptance even if PDF is used at
  initial submission.
- The manuscript should use double line spacing, line and page numbering, SI
  units and no manually inserted page breaks.
- Accepted main-manuscript formats include DOC/DOCX, RTF and TeX/LaTeX.

## Limits not stated by the official guideline

The archived official Research guideline does not specify a manuscript word
limit, a numerical abstract word limit, or a total cap on figures and tables.
Secondary claims such as `5000-8000 words`, `250-word abstract` or `10-15 total
figures/tables` must not be presented as JEAS rules. The current portal may
impose fields or updated constraints after login and must be checked immediately
before upload.
